type_IV/review/ -- programs written for the second internal review of Theorem D (Section 7 of the note)
========================================================================================================

These programs were written separately from the main code in type_IV/ and share no code with it. They were run on
6 October 2026 (Python 3 with NumPy, SymPy and python-flint).

  periods_check.py SEED -> periods_check.out        (run: python -u periods_check.py 20261006)
      20 random points of the period domain over 10 pairs (d,m). Exact checks of Lemma 7.1, of the element a_0 of
      Theorem D(ii) (both positivity conventions) and of the signatures; consequences of Proposition 7.2: the Hodge
      circle lies in Lie(G_H), not in Lie(R_{F/Q} SU(H)), and generates Lie(G_H) (dimension 17) as an ideal of
      Lie(U_L(H)). Expected last line: "SUMMARY: 20 cases, all ok = True".

  hodge_ring_check.py N OUTFILE -> hodge_ring_check_k3.out, hodge_ring_check_k4.out
      (run: python -u hodge_ring_check.py 4 hodge_ring_check_k4.out; about 10 s)
      Hodge ring of A^k for the group G_H, k <= N: per dominant weight, highest-weight multiplicities against skew
      Howe duality, and membership of every highest-weight vector in the algebra generated by divisor classes and
      pull-backs of Weil classes (ranks modulo 2^31-1). Part A does this on each of the two sides (the two factors
      SL_3 of G_H over C) separately; Part B compares the dimensions with ../hodge_dims.out; Part C is a direct
      two-sided computation for k = 1, 2. For k >= 3 the products of classes from the two sides are not checked.
      Expected last line: "ALL OK".

  cm_points_check.py -> cm_points_check.out
      CM types of L.C (C a real cyclic cubic field) with the multiplicities of Theorem D: dimension of the Hodge
      group and the example of Remark 7.3 (dim Hg = 5).
